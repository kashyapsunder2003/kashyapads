Branded Search · Negative Keyword Conflict

From $300 CPL to $40: Fixing a Negative Keyword Crisis

A branded search campaign had a CPL of $300, far above any acceptable target. Account-level negative keywords were inadvertently blocking the campaign's own branded terms, preventing high-intent users from seeing the ads entirely. Traffic was being routed into expensive, irrelevant queries. Once the conflicting negatives were identified and restructured, branded traffic returned and CPL dropped from $300 to $40.

Lead Gen Campaign · Conversion Tracking Fix

CPL Was $180. The Real Problem Was What We Were Tracking

A lead generation campaign was reporting a CPL of $180 with no path to improvement. The audit revealed the tracking was firing on the wrong event, a non-lead company action, while the actual inquiry form that real prospects were filling was completely untracked. Smart Bidding had been optimising toward the wrong signal the entire time. Once tracking was corrected to the actual lead form, the algorithm found real high-intent users and CPL dropped significantly.
